Paces are Tom “Tinman” Schwartz's training zones. Each race becomes a velocity at VO2max, and every zone is a fixed fraction of it, so the paces follow the VO2 cost curve rather than a straight line between fitness levels — runners above or below the published range stay on the same curve. Ranges show fast–slow. CV 1000 and CV 800 are the CV mile at a shorter distance, so all three always agree. Each athlete's season best is their best-fitness performance at the selected distance within the chosen season (a season is the calendar year the race was run). Class years are taken from the result row, falling back to the school's athlete roster, and are shown for the season the race was run in. Race Pace is what the athlete actually ran — the season best straight off the clock, per mile and per kilometre.
