Paces use the Tinman/Critical-Velocity model, calibrated to Final Surge across the fitness range. Each athlete's season best is their best-fitness performance at the selected distance within the chosen season (a season is the calendar year the race was run — the same filter the rankings page uses). Class years are taken from the result row, falling back to the school's athlete roster, and are shown for the season the race was run in. Ranges show fast–slow. Race Pace is what the athlete actually ran — the season best straight off the clock, per mile and per kilometre.
